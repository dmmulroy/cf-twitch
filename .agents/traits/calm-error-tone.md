---
name: calm-error-tone
subject: Wording of error messages.
requirement: Error messages use calm, direct, ordinary language that describes
  the problem without blaming the caller or a third party.
purpose: Keeps failures serious and understandable for the audience reading them.
---
Calm: `The photo is larger than 12 MB. Choose a smaller photo and try again.`

Not calm: `Oops!! You broke it 😅 — your upload was WAY too big.`

Which words suit the audience is covered by `durable-public-vocabulary`.
